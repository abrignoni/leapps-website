# Everybody Gets a Release!

**Short version:** iLEAPP, ALEAPP, RLEAPP, and VLEAPP are all at v2026.2.0. LAVA is at v0.14.0. Go get them, they are free and they are ready.

**Long version:** grab a coffee, because this one is big. Five tools moved at once, and every single one of them got capabilities that will change what you can pull out of an extraction. Let me walk you through the parts I am most excited about.

## iLEAPP v2026.2.0

Remember Biome? Those SQLite databases and Set.db stores that recent iOS versions quietly started keeping? They are parsed now. Per-launch app openings, installed apps, contacts, FindMy devices, significant locations, Shortcut phrases, plus the SEGB streams for harvested mail and messages, reads, screen time, and keyboard activity. That is a brand new pattern-of-life source that nobody was touching a month ago.

Photos.sqlite got a full rework with iOS 26 support, courtesy of the awesome Scott Koenig. If you do iOS photo forensics, this is the artifact family you live in, and it is current with the newest OS. Forty two artifacts, all of them modernized.

Then there is C2PA Content Provenance. Was that picture generated by AI? iLEAPP now reads Content Credentials manifests and tells you, including the signer certificate and the signing time. Given where the world is going, having that in a free tool feels pretty good.

One more thing worth calling out, because it matters: Notes was returning zero rows on iOS 16 and later. Zero. The account foreign key had quietly moved. It is fixed. Go re-run your old cases.

Get it here: [iLEAPP](https://www.leapps.org/releases#section-ileapp)

## ALEAPP v2026.2.0

Samsung Honeyboard clipboard, reworked with WAL recovery. Read that again. Deleted clipboard entries, recovered from the write-ahead log, with SEFT support and dual-schema handling so it works across versions. Clipboards are where people paste the things they do not want to type twice, and now you can get at the ones they thought were gone.

Mister Skinnylegs is in ALEAPP, contributed by software developer extraordinaire Alex Caithness.

C2PA Content Provenance landed here too, so AI image detection is not an iOS-only party. Same signer certificate parsing, same Media State column grouping manifests per file.

Under the hood, the entire codebase finished its context-form migration. Over two hundred files. That is not a headline feature, but it is the reason the next two hundred artifacts will be easier to write and easier to trust.

Get it here: [ALEAPP](https://www.leapps.org/releases#section-aleapp)

## RLEAPP v2026.2.0

The Snapchat overhaul is the star. Snapchat changed the format of their legal returns, which is the sort of thing that silently breaks everybody's parsing. RLEAPP now maps those CSV artifacts by header name instead of position, so a reordered column does not take the whole thing down. Conversation parsing is fixed, and there are new Conversation List, My AI Conversations, and Call Logs artifacts, with conversation view and direction columns.

New parsers arrived in a pile: Facebook Messenger, ChatGPT data exports and user info, MeWe with ten artifacts, Omega Chat, and a Synchronoss / Verizon Cloud legal return parser with nine more. If you work returns, your coverage just went way up.

And RLEAPP hit one hundred percent LAVA coverage. Every artifact, modern pipeline, extraction-relative source paths.

Get it here: [RLEAPP](https://www.leapps.org/releases#section-rleapp)

## VLEAPP v2026.2.0

Vehicle forensics finished its LAVA migration. All of it. Alfa Romeo, Chrysler, Ford, Hyundai with seven telematics artifacts, Nissan Altima, and Nuance VCA, plus the PAS debug-log parsers and the tar_checker and sal_data artifacts. The whole toolkit is on the modern pipeline.

There is also a fix I love because it is so boring and so important: files with glob metacharacters in the name, like IMG_0347.jpg, now resolve properly. That bracket used to make a file invisible. Not anymore.

Cars are the most mobile data sources we have, and getting this toolkit current has been a long haul. Very happy to see it done.

Get it here: [VLEAPP](https://www.leapps.org/releases#section-vleapp)

## LAVA v0.14.0

Timestamps got serious attention: explicit UTC labeling, better timezone formatting and controls, and a fix for dates rendering without a time. When your whole case is a timeline, ambiguous timestamps are not a small thing.

The welcome window now reads your recent LEAPP runs, so your last outputs are right there when you open the app. James Habben wrote that one up on this very blog if you want the details.

Also, LAVA speaks Italian now, alongside the German updates. And yes, there are zodiac indicators on the date popovers. Because why not.

Get it here: [LAVA](https://www.leapps.org/releases#section-lava)
